# Community conduct

Help people understand and improve Walkie One. Be respectful, explain technical
disagreements with evidence, and give contributors room to learn. The same
standard applies to issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, and publishing
someone else's private information are unacceptable. Critique the work rather
than attacking the person. Respect a request to stop an unwelcome interaction.

Report conduct concerns privately to [contact@walkieone.com](mailto:contact@walkieone.com)
with the subject **Walkie One conduct report**. The Santa’s AI Lab maintainers
will review reports, limit sharing to people needed to handle them, and consider
both the conduct and its impact. Do not publish private correspondence or
personal information as part of a public dispute.

Maintainers may ask for a correction, remove content, restrict participation
temporarily, or ban a participant for serious or repeated misconduct. They
should explain the action privately where practical and apply this standard
consistently. A person affected by a moderation decision can request a second
maintainer's review through the same contact address.

Security vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Ordinary technical
disagreement, constructive criticism and good-faith bug reports are welcome.
